## Getting Started

### What is RBLX Clothing Maker?
RBLX Clothing Maker is a mobile app for designing Roblox **classic clothing** — shirts and pants. You can draw, use templates and stickers, preview on a 3D avatar, save projects, and share designs with the community.

**Note:** We are not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.

### How do I create an account?
1. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**
2. Enter email, username, and password
3. Verify your email if prompted
4. Complete onboarding and start designing

### How do I create my first design?
1. Tap **Create** or choose a template from **Templates**
2. Pick **Shirt** or **Pants**
3. Use the editor tools (brush, eraser, layers, stickers)
4. Preview on the avatar
5. Tap **Save** to store your project

---

## Editor

### What are layers?
Layers let you draw on separate sheets (e.g. base, details, logo). You can show/hide, lock, and reorder layers.

### How do I upload an image?
Use the **Image / Import** tool in the editor. Supported formats include PNG and JPG. Make sure you have rights to use any image you upload.

### How do I preview my design?
Open the **3D Preview** panel to see your clothing on an avatar. Classic clothing uses Roblox UV layout — what you see in the editor maps to the in-game appearance.

### Grid and UV template
Toggle the **grid** for alignment. The orange outline shows the active UV template region for shirt or pants.

## Saving & Projects

### Where are my designs saved?
Designs are saved to your account in the cloud. Sign in on any device with the same account to access them.

### How do I delete a design?
Go to **Profile → My Designs**, open the design menu, and choose **Delete**.

---

## Community

### How do I publish a design?
From your saved design, use **Publish to Community**. Public designs appear in **Explore** where others can view, like, save, or remix (if enabled).

### Can I make a design private again?
Unpublish or delete the community post from your profile, depending on available options in the app.

### What is Remix?
Remix lets you start from another user's public design as a base. Always respect original creators and copyright.

## Roblox

### How do I upload to Roblox?
1. Export or download your finished template from the app
2. Go to [Roblox Create](https://create.roblox.com) → **Avatar** → **Classic Clothing**
3. Upload your PNG following Roblox's guidelines
4. Set price and publish on Roblox

We do not upload to Roblox on your behalf. You must follow **Roblox Terms of Use** and **Community Standards**.

### How do I link my Roblox account?
Go to **Settings → Link Roblox** and follow the steps. Linking is optional and helps organize your workflow.

## Subscriptions & Credits

### What is Premium?
Premium may include unlimited saves, extra templates/stickers, export options, or other features shown on the paywall. Exact benefits are listed in-app at purchase time.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
**Android:** Google Play Store → Profile → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions** → RBLX Clothing Maker → **Cancel**

**iOS:** Settings → Apple ID → **Subscriptions** → RBLX Clothing Maker → **Cancel**

Cancellation stops future charges; you keep access until the current period ends.

### I was charged but didn't receive credits
1. Restart the app and wait a few minutes
2. Check you're signed in with the same account used for purchase
3. Tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings or Paywall (if available)
4. Email **support@hun.social** with your receipt ID

## Troubleshooting

### App won't open or crashes
- Update to the latest version from the store
- Restart your device
- Clear app cache (Android: Settings → Apps → RBLX Clothing Maker → Storage)
- Reinstall the app (your cloud saves remain if you sign back in)

### Login issues
- Check internet connection
- Reset password via **Forgot password**
- Confirm email verification if required

### Designs not syncing
- Check you're online and signed in
- Pull to refresh on Profile
- Sign out and sign back in

### Purchase not working
- Ensure Google Play / App Store is up to date
- Use the same Google/Apple account as your store purchases
- Try **Restore Purchases**
