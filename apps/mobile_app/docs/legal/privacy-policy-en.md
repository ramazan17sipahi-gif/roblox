By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Username
- Password (stored securely; we never store plain-text passwords)

### 2.2 User content
When you use the App, we may store:
- Clothing designs and projects you create
- Uploaded images
- Layer and editor data
- Designs you publish to the community
- Likes, saves, and remix activity

### 2.3 Linked accounts
If you choose to link a Roblox account, we store the connection data needed to associate your designs with that account. We do not receive your Roblox password.

### 2.4 Purchase information
Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by **Google Play** (Android) or **Apple App Store** (iOS). We receive purchase status and product identifiers to unlock features or credits. We do **not** receive your full payment card details.

### 2.5 Device and usage data
We may automatically collect:
- Device type and operating system
- App version
- Crash logs and performance data (to fix bugs)
- General usage events (e.g. feature usage)

We do not sell your personal information.
